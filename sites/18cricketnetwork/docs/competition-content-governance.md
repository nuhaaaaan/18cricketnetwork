# Competition authority and content safety

Competition creators are administrators by ownership. Only a creator can appoint/remove registered accounts by their verified identity email. Tournament authority includes the tournament creator, appointed tournament admins, and its parent league's creator/admins. Roles selected during onboarding do not grant administrative powers.

Match owners record live deliveries. In competition matches, undo and umpire correction commands, scoring setup, completed-scorecard corrections, and fixture edits create pending requests. Only a current competition admin can approve/reject. The proposal retains the prior snapshot, source version, submitter, reviewer and timestamps. Approval checks the source version atomically with applying the change. Stale requests must be rejected and resubmitted. Match scope cannot be detached through fixture edits. Competition scorecards cannot be deleted through the ordinary record endpoint. Standalone practice matches remain controlled by their owner.

The Competition & safety workspace creates leagues and linked tournaments/matches, manages admin appointments, submits fixture corrections and reviews requests. Competition admins are separate from platform content moderators, whose authority comes from the server's PLATFORM_ADMIN_EMAIL setting.

Routine posts, media listings and comments publish immediately after automatic text checks. Clear sensitive identifier, political propaganda, hate/violence, sexual and profanity patterns return 422 before the content is saved. Safe edits also publish immediately. Legacy social/media records containing these patterns are automatically hidden and marked rejected when read; prohibited legacy comments are excluded. This is a limited keyword screen, not a claim of exhaustive multilingual detection.

Public image uploads require OPENAI_API_KEY for two automatic checks before storage: omni-moderation-latest plus a visual platform-policy classifier (OPENAI_SAFETY_MODEL, default gpt-4.1-mini) covering political campaigning, profanity, hate symbols and sensitive identifiers. Flagged images return 422; missing credentials, provider outages or malformed decisions return 503 without persisting the upload. Images are limited to 10 MB. Direct external social image URLs are rejected because their content can change outside this gate. Videos return 415 until a video/audio screening pipeline is implemented. Older unscanned assets remain inaccessible to other visitors. Platform operators can remove already published text and handle appeals against an exact version. Manual approval cannot bypass uploaded-media classification.

Private PDF carrier documents retain participant-only access and receive text screening where text can be extracted from their raw bytes; compressed PDF content is not fully classified. Ownership-proof PDFs/JPGs/PNGs use a separate private purpose and are accessible only to their registered uploader and platform verifier. They cannot be repurposed as public product or social images. Evidence verification is mandatory for an ownership claim, not a general hold on activity. Automated classifiers can miss content; continuous live-stream moderation, video/audio classification, malware scanning and stronger PDF text extraction remain launch dependencies.

API regression tests use isolated in-memory databases, not published sample data. Browser interaction verification is pending because the prescribed browser tooling is unavailable in this environment.
